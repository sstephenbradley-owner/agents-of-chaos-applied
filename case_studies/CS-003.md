# CS-003: Not a distinct run

**Evidence status:** no separate record. The label describes the same run as CS-001.

The case-study inventory (March 13, 2026) and the method paper (Section 4.3) describe a "CS-003" as a cross-model red team of a chapter on regulatory capture: three rounds, two Gemini chats with asymmetric context, reliability 3.5 to 8.0, and three errors (securities-backed credit mechanics, a steel company's historical profit figures, and an NDAA legislative timeline). The inventory dates it "Multiple sessions, February to March 2026".

No session matching that description exists apart from CS-001:

- Every listed detail (three rounds, two chats, context asymmetry, 3.5 to 8.0, securities-backed credit error, steel-profit discrepancy, NDAA timeline) appears in the March 12, 2026 session that is CS-001.
- That session saved a one-paragraph case-study note listing exactly those three errors plus the score arc.
- The inventory was built on March 13 after the author chose to have the inventory built from existing material, with transcripts to be added later. So the inventory came from saved notes and session context, not transcripts. Its CS-001 entry took the five error categories from the paper draft; its CS-003 entry matches the saved note.
- The inventory called CS-003 complete on March 13, so any separate run must predate that. A search of the full chat export for conversations started from February 1 to March 13, 2026 found these markers (securities-backed credit, the steel-profit figures, "3.5/10") only in the March 12 CS-001 session, the March 13 session that wrote the inventory, and a March 8 manuscript review. In that review the securities-backed credit figures were checked and found correct, with no scores or rounds. Later conversations that mention the same markers postdate the inventory.

## Consequences for published claims

- Claims that the same error categories "recurred across unrelated documents" in CS-001 and CS-003 are not supported. There was one document.
- Averages "across CS-001 and CS-003" count one run twice.
- The method paper's CS-003 score tags ([DATA:SCORE_CS003_*]) cannot be filled from a separate record. The real figures are the CS-001 scores.

## Recommendation for the repository

Retire the CS-003 label. Cite CS-001 for this run. If a new regulatory-capture chapter is run through the protocol later, give it a new ID.
